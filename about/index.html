About RimWorld Wiki

RimWorld Wiki is an unofficial fan-made GameWikiHub archive for practical colony survival guidance: colonists, raids, base design, research, farming, medical systems, DLC, mods and advanced strategy.

The site is built as a fast static knowledge base with consistent article templates, searchable content, SEO metadata and non-invasive ad placements.

Editorial approach

Articles are written like advice from a veteran colony player: direct, tactical and focused on preventing avoidable disasters.
