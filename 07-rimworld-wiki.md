# 07-rimworld-wiki.md

# RimWorld Wiki Content Expansion Project

## Project Objective

Transform the RimWorld wiki from a database of game information into an expert strategy resource.

Google already understands:

- item names
- weapon stats
- research trees
- faction descriptions

The goal is to explain:

- what experienced players recommend
- common mistakes
- colony optimization
- DLC decision making
- strategic tradeoffs

Every article should help players make decisions.

---

# Content Philosophy

Write like a RimWorld veteran with 500+ hours.

Avoid:

- encyclopedia writing
- robotic explanations
- simple stat repetition
- patch-note style summaries

Prefer:

- recommendations
- strategy
- colony planning
- risk analysis
- player experience

Every article should answer:

"What would an experienced player actually do?"
