# RimWorld Wiki Project Blueprint

A complete planning and content structure document for building a RimWorld-focused standalone wiki site similar in quality and structure to the Project Zomboid wiki project.

Target domain example:
`https://rimworld.gamewikihub.com/`

---

# 1. Core Project Identity

## Site Purpose
The RimWorld wiki should feel like:
- A survival colony management handbook
- A sci‑fi storytelling encyclopedia
- A practical strategy archive
- A modding and DLC reference center

The site should prioritize:
- Fast loading
- Readability
- Long-form guides
- Searchable systems
- High SEO value
- Mobile friendliness
- Strong atmosphere

The tone should feel:
- Harsh
- Frontier sci‑fi
- Industrial
- Tactical
- Story-driven
- Survival-focused

Unlike polished futuristic sci-fi, RimWorld should feel:
- improvised
- dangerous
- dusty
- practical
- colony-built

---
